# Vision

## Promise

A real interview with a real person within **48 hours** of signing up, and a clear list of what to fix next — **free forever**.

## The problem

| Gap | What happens | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fear of being watched | Freshers freeze on camera before reaching the questions | Assumption — test H1 in Phase 0 |
| No one to practise with | Seniors/teachers busy; fresher networks small; weeks to arrange a mock | Plausible — test H2, H3 |
| Generic feedback | Question lists ignore projects; rich feedback often paid | Assumption — re-check competitors before publishing claim |
| One side of the table | Freshers rarely learn to ask questions or judge answers | Assumption — reciprocity design tests it |

**Context (checked 28 Oct 2026):** India Skills Report 2026 — national employability **56.35%**, BE/BTech **70.15%**. Use for scale of need, not to promise that OIN raises placement rates. Sources: AffairsCloud, Careers360, The CSR Journal (see proposal Appendix F).

Audience focus: capable students in tier-2 / tier-3 cities with fewer mentors.

## Positioning

> For students in India who need realistic interview practice, OIN is the free, open-source practice network where real people interview you against a fair rubric, and software on your own device covers the gaps. Unlike AI-only tools it builds interviewing skill on both sides of the table, and unlike paid expert services it costs nothing and keeps your data with you.

**Do not sell “AI mock interviews.”** AI avatar interviewers and full-drive simulators already exist for Indian campuses.

**Sell:** trained humans, public rubric, free cohort tools, privacy-first comfort ladder, open source + self-hosting.

**Moat (strength order):** institution trust → trained interviewer community → consented human-rated evaluation dataset → OSS contributors → brand.

## Design rules (“real, not generic”)

1. Humans are the product; software is scaffolding (≥60% practice minutes with humans by month 3 — hypothesis).
2. Real interviewers — kit-trained, certified, badged.
3. Real drive patterns — cards tagged by company type/round; moderated experience notes; no leaked papers.
4. Real conditions — timers, follow-ups, pressure ladder; comfort ladder ends at real video.
5. Real feedback — public rubric + evidence; candidate self-scores first; AI-written text labelled.
6. Real language — Hindi UI from pilot; Hinglish only after accuracy bar.
7. Real places — weekly Mock Interview Night online **and** on campus.
8. Real outcomes — opt-in shortlist/offer follow-up; certificates show hours/growth, never a job promise; no recruiter profile access except one shared profile.

## What free cannot give you

1. No SLA — reliability from design (fallbacks, backups, quotas), not provider promises.
2. Capacity ceiling ~1,000–3,000 MAU and ~100 simultaneous sessions per free deployment (estimate).
3. Groups capped at **4** on free path (5–6 need optional node).
4. AI is lighter — rubric/evidence-based; live AI interviewer is turn-based.
5. Domain, lawyer, company, trademark cost money — free routes exist with trade-offs.
6. People are the real cost — volunteer burnout &gt; servers.
7. Optional free VMs (e.g. Oracle) are **not** on the critical path.
