# Interview rubric (scores 1–4)

Publish and **version** this rubric. Each score needs evidence from the session. Use **not assessed** when a criterion did not come up.

| Criterion | 1 Not yet | 2 Developing | 3 Solid | 4 Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Structure (STAR) | Rambling, no clear start or end | Some order, missing role or result | Clear situation, action and result | Clear STAR plus a lesson and a concrete outcome |
| Specificity | General claims only | A few details | Named projects, tools and numbers | Detail that holds up under follow-ups |
| Ownership | Cannot say what they did | Unclear split between self and team | Clear personal contribution | Clear contribution, trade-offs and what they would change |
| Technical correctness | Major errors | Right idea with gaps | Mostly correct, minor gaps | Correct, with edge cases and trade-offs |
| Problem solving | No approach | Needs heavy hints | Reasonable approach, light hints | Structured approach that checks its own answer |
| Communication | Hard to follow | Understandable with effort | Clear and well paced | Clear, concise, adapts to the listener |
| Conduct and listening | Interrupts or defensive | Sometimes misses the question | Answers the question asked | Listens, clarifies and builds on follow-ups |

## Interviewer practice scores

Question quality, follow-ups, note-taking, fairness.

## Group discussion scores

Initiative, content, listening, leadership, conclusion.

## Coach engine scope

The Coach proposes values only for criteria it can measure (structure, specificity, ownership signals, coverage, communication metrics). Others stay **not assessed** until a person scores them.
